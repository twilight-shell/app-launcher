# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version
may change how things work.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- The launcher, "Twilight's Library": a spellbook card with search, apps with their icons, keyboard and mouse.
- Search that finds an app typed on the Russian keyboard layout, by name, by word, by generic name, keyword or category, or by its letters in order.
- The terminal that runs terminal apps is a setting; the settings window has a page for it.
- Tests for the search.
